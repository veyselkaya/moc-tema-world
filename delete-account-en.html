Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: May 12, 2026

You can delete your MOC Tema World account and all associated personal data at any time. Two methods are described below.

Method 1 — In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the MOC Tema World app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete My Account".
  4. Enter your password on the confirmation screen to complete the deletion.

Method 2 — Request via Email

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer a manual process, send a deletion request from your registered email address to:

Email: atakentmoc@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Body: Please include the email address associated with your account and the sentence "I request the deletion of my account and all my data."

Your request will be processed within 30 days.

Data That Is Deleted

Once your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account information (email, name, user ID)
  • Order history and loyalty program (stars/wallet) data
  • In-app preferences and session information
  • Push notification token (FCM token)

Data That May Be Retained

Due to legal obligations (e.g. tax law, invoice records), certain financial records may be retained for a limited period in an anonymized form, stripped of personally identifying information.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is irreversible. Your star balance, rewards and order history cannot be restored after deletion.
